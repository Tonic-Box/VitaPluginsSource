# Vita Bank Valuer
Shows where the value in your bank is: a side panel lists your most valuable bank stacks, by Grand Exchange price times quantity.
- coins count at face value, platinum tokens at 1,000 each
- items with no price are left out
- updates when your bank changes, and on logout

### How to use
1. Enable the plugin and open the **Bank Valuer** panel from the sidebar.
2. Open your bank once so the client knows what is in it.

### Settings
In the panel:
- **Items to show** (1 to 50, default 10): how many stacks to list.
- **Hide untradeables**: leave out items that can't be traded.
- **Refresh**: values the bank again.
