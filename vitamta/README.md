# Vita MTA
Plays the Mage Training Arena for pizazz points: Telekinetic Theatre, Alchemists' Playground, Enchanting Chamber and Creature Graveyard.

### How to use
Bring the runes and staves for the rooms you want, enable the plugin and switch on **Run**. Start anywhere; it banks any coins and walks to the arena. Rooms you can't cast for are skipped, and it swaps to the staff or tome that uses the fewest runes.

### Settings
- **Rooms**: which rooms to play.
- **Room choice**: Reward target, Lowest points first, or a Single room.
- **Reward target** / **When reached**: the item to earn points for, and whether to stack points, stop, or buy it.
- **Alchemy**: High, Low, or best castable.
- **Creature Graveyard**: when to eat and when to leave on low HP.
