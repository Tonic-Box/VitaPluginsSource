# Vita Tempoross
Plays the Tempoross skilling boss on mass worlds for you.
- fishes
- cooks 
- loads harpoonfish
- tethers through waves
- puts out fires
- steps out from under storm clouds
- harpoons the spirit pool

### How to use
1. Bring a harpoon (plain, barb-tail, dragon, infernal or crystal), worn or carried, and the gear you want
2. Enable the plugin and switch on **Run**
   - if you are not at the docks it will walk you there
   - If you are not on world 422 it will hop you there
   - It will not start without a harpoon or below 35 Fishing.

### Settings
- **Strategy**: Cook for permits, or Raw for XP.
- **Fish per load**: how many fish to catch before cooking and loading; 0 picks 7, or 9 at 85 Fishing.
- **Load below energy** / **Emergency load at storm**: when to load early.
- **Use harpoon special**: the dragon, infernal or crystal harpoon's special before fishing.
- **Water buckets** / **Take a hammer**: supplies taken in the cove.
- **Collect at permits**: search the reward pool and bank the loot once this many permits are held; 0 never.
- **Max runtime** / **Stop after games**: when to switch Run off.
